# Contractor Money Tools — Product

## Product concept

Contractor Money Tools is an English-language web product for U.S. home-service business owners and contractors.

It helps business owners understand what employees, labor hours, jobs, overhead, equipment, and growth decisions actually cost.

The product is tool-first rather than article-first.

## Initial audience

Small and owner-operated home-service businesses, including:

- HVAC
- plumbing
- electrical
- roofing
- remodeling
- landscaping
- painting
- cleaning
- pest control
- similar field-service businesses

## Core user questions

The product should help answer questions such as:

- What does this employee really cost me?
- What should I charge per billable hour?
- What markup gives me my target margin?
- Did I actually make money on this job?
- Can I afford another employee?
- How much overhead does each billable hour need to recover?

## MVP calculators

1. Labor Burden Calculator
2. True Employee Cost Calculator
3. Billable Hour Rate Calculator
4. Contractor Markup Calculator
5. Job Profitability Calculator

## Long-term direction

The calculators may eventually become a connected Contractor Economics Engine where values can flow between related tools.

Example:

Employee wage
→ True labor cost
→ Required billable rate
→ Job profitability

## MVP philosophy

Keep the first version small.

Do not build:
- accounts
- payments
- premium subscriptions
- CRM functionality
- mobile apps
- AI chatbots
- large content libraries

until real usage validates the core calculators.

## Primary success signals

- calculator starts
- calculator completions
- movement between related calculators
- repeat visitors
- organic discovery
- useful feedback from contractors

Article count is not a success metric.

## Initial market

United States.

## Monetization

Monetization is deliberately postponed until usage is validated.

Potential future models include:
- software affiliate partnerships
- qualified leads
- sponsorships
- premium tools
- B2B partnerships

These should not influence MVP calculator methodology or results.
